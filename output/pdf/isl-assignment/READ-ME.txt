NETWORK INTERPRETATION
Paper nodes are linked by shared authors, not by citations. Names are
normalized for case and diacritics. Density: 14.33%; mean degree: 3.44.
The corpus is a purposive selection, not an exhaustive systematic review.
Record years can differ from later conference/proceedings versions.

and the two actual screenshots. It also renders the report for inspection.
